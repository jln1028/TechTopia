Five interactive lessons. Take them in order.

| # | Lesson | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Using Your pi-top [4]](../TechTopia_Industry_Challenge_Intro_Lessons_SenseHat/1-using-your-pitop4.html) | Powering on, miniscreen, desktop, file manager, settings, Foundation and Expansion Plate |
